# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 4, 2026**

This privacy policy applies to the browser extension that converts genealogy tree data displayed on supported websites into a GEDCOM file.

## Data Access

The extension may access genealogy information displayed on the supported website, such as names, dates, family relationships, and other information required to create a GEDCOM file.

This access occurs only for the purpose of performing the conversion requested by the user.

## Data Collection

The extension does not collect, store, sell, or share users' personal information.

Genealogy data processed by the extension is handled locally in the user's browser and is not transmitted to the extension developer or to any external server.

## Data Storage

The extension does not maintain a database of user information or genealogy data.

Generated GEDCOM files are created for the user and are saved or downloaded according to the user's browser settings.

## Third-Party Websites

The extension interacts with third-party websites in order to read genealogy information that is available to the logged-in user.

The extension is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the operators of those websites.

Use of third-party websites is subject to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

## Permissions

Browser permissions requested by the extension are used only when necessary to provide its functionality, such as accessing the genealogy tree page and generating the GEDCOM file.

The extension does not use these permissions for advertising, tracking, or unrelated purposes.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data-handling practices change.
